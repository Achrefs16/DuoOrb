## 7. Game truth (what content must say — engine-verified)

- **Board:** 9×9 cells, 8×8 wall lattice, 128 slots (`constants.ts:3-4`, `geometry.ts:43-48`).
- **Walls/modes:** 1v1 = 10 each; 3p center = 6; 4p = 5 (`constants.ts:30-42`). Modes `2p/4p/race2-4/center2-3` (`types.ts:38-45`). Custom rooms allow 10/15/99 (`MatchSetupScreen.tsx:19,447-468`).
- **Turn:** one pawn step (orthogonal) XOR one wall (permanent, 2-square, no overlap/cross, `geometry.ts:115-155`).
- **Jumps:** adjacent orb → straight jump if clear behind (mandatory); else diagonal(s) — triggers on wall, edge, **or occupied-behind** (`movement.ts:88-122`).
- **Golden rule:** wall must leave every ACTIVE player a path (`walls.ts:46-62`).
- **Goals:** edge rows/cols normally; center modes (`4p/center2/center3`) win on **(4,4)** only (`pathfinding.ts:11-56`).
- **Starts:** 1v1 `(8,4)→TOP` vs `(0,4)→BOTTOM`; 4p adds `(4,0)→RIGHT`, `(4,8)→LEFT`; race spreads bottom row (`ruleset.ts:80-182`).
- **Notation:** `Bd5` pawns, `Hd4` walls, seats B/R/G/Y (`notation.ts:52-82`).
- **Bots (7, weakest-first, `personalities.ts:89-615`):** Martin 800, Elena 1100, Nelson 1450, Sofia 1650, Marcus 1900, Viktor 2100, Achref 2400 + generic Easy 1200 / Normal 1500 / Hard 1800 (`MatchSetupScreen.tsx:60-64`). **All free** (UI `MatchSetupScreen.tsx:289`; data flag `premium:true` is stale; `MONETIZATION.md` 6-bot Pip/Vex roster is stale — aliases preserved `:617-626`).
- **Clocks (7):** 1+0, 1+1, 3+0, 3+2, 5+0, 5+3, 10+0, default 3+0 (`timeControls.ts:15-25,48`); setup UI shows first 3.
- **Rooms:** 6-letter code from unambiguous alphabet (`room.service.ts:31-38`); invite `https://duoorb.com/?room=CODE` (`OnlineScreen.tsx:399`); `en.ts:478` “Enter 6-letter room code”.
- **Analysis:** Rust MCTS `duoorb-rust-mcts-2.0`, grades BEST→BLUNDER (`analysis.ts:21-107`, `classifier.rs:12-62`).

The Four Simple Rules That Create Infinite Depth
The genius of the game is that a child can learn it in two minutes, but grandmasters study the tactics for years.
The Objective
1. The Cross-Board Race
You start on your baseline; your opponent starts on the opposite side. The first piece to touch any space on the rival's starting edge wins.
Your Choice
2. Step or Build
Every turn, you do only one thing: move your piece one step, OR place a 2-square wall into the board's grooves to force a detour.
The Leap
3. Jumping Rivals
When you meet face-to-face with no wall between you, you can jump cleanly over your rival. If a wall blocks behind them, you leap diagonally!
The Catch
4. No Complete Traps
You can make your rival's path 30 steps long, but you can never completely close all exits. At least one open path to the goal must always exist.

DuoOrb is a tactical grid-strategy game by AS Digital. The app talks to our game server for accounts, matchmaking, friends, history and ratings. Auth via Supabase (Google sign-in + guest sessions). Account identifiers (user ID, username, display name, email for Google accounts); player content (bio, avatar); game activity (matches, moves, Glicko-2 ratings, achievements); social graph (friends, blocks); premium status (no card details — via Google Play/RevenueCat); diagnostics (hashed IP, user-agent, crash logs); advertising (AdSense cookies on web, AdMob advertising ID in app, under Google's policies). No precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. No in-game chat.
