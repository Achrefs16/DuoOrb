Cookies and ads

DuoOrb web pages show Google AdSense page ads. Google uses cookies to serve, measure and personalize ads. Matches themselves are never interrupted by popups.

Manage ads

Control personalization in your Google ad settings and browser cookie controls. Premium in the mobile app removes ads; web pages remain ad-supported. Questions: support@duoorb.com. See Privacy.

Take control in 3 steps

1. Open your browser's cookie settings to block or clear third-party cookies at any time. 2. Visit Google's ad settings to switch ad personalization on or off across the web. 3. In the EEA, UK or Switzerland, re-open the consent message on any page to change your IAB TCF choices — your decision is stored for 12 months and never blocks gameplay.

Cookies & Local Storage Used on DuoOrb.com

We use first-party session storage to operate the game interface and Google AdSense cookies (subject to your consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland) to serve, cap, and measure advertisements on our informational web pages.

Name Provider Type Duration Purpose
sb-*-auth-token Supabase Auth (First-Party) localStorage Persistent until logout (180d guest cap) Strictly necessary authentication token that preserves your guest session or signed-in Google account across matches.
__gads Google AdSense HTTP Cookie 13 Months Advertising measurement: enables websites to show Google ads, measures ad interactions, and prevents the same ad from displaying too many times.
__gpi Google AdSense HTTP Cookie 13 Months Google Publisher Identifier: collects information on visitor behavior across ad-supported pages to optimize ad delivery and frequency capping.
