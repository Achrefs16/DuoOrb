Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · DuoOrb by AS Digital. Contact: support@duoorb.com.

1. Who we are

DuoOrb is a tactical grid-strategy game by AS Digital, playable free in any web browser and on native mobile apps. The app talks to our game server for accounts, matchmaking, rooms, friends, history, cosmetics and ratings. Auth via Supabase (Google sign-in + guest sessions). Post-game engine analysis runs locally on your device. Free play on web shows Google AdSense page ads; free play in the app shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads for Hints, Undos, bot unlocks, and extra match reviews). DuoOrb Premium removes all ads and unlocks all 39 AI bots, 16 Ball & Wall cosmetic sets, 6 Hints & 4 Undos per bot match, unlimited Engine Match Reviews, and the subscriber Diamond badge.

2. Data we collect

Account identifiers (user ID, username, display name, email for Google accounts); player content & customization (bio, avatar, badge showcase, equipped Ball & Wall cosmetic skins); game activity (matches, moves, Glicko-2 ratings in Classical 1v1, achievements, AI wins, bot mastery stars and unlocked bots); social graph (friends, blocks, room participation); premium status (no card details — via Google Play/RevenueCat); diagnostics (hashed IP, user-agent, crash logs); advertising (AdSense cookies on web, AdMob advertising ID in app, under Google's policies). No precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. No free-form in-game chat (preset emoji reactions only).

3. Cookies and advertising

Our web pages load Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization in Google's ad settings. See Cookies. Consent is requested where required before ads load.

4. Sharing

Processors only: (a) Supabase for authentication (email, auth tokens via Google sign-in and guest sessions minted by our server); (b) our hosting provider for the game server and database (account, game and social rows needed to run the service); (c) Google AdSense on web and Google AdMob in the app (advertising ID, cookies and ad-interaction data, under Google's own policies); (d) Google Play Billing via RevenueCat (subscription status only — no card or payment details ever touch our servers). We never sell personal data and never share it with data brokers. Reactions during matches are ephemeral and never stored.

5. Security

All client-server traffic uses HTTPS (TLS) and secure WebSockets. Passwords are never handled by us (Google OAuth via Supabase). Guest refresh tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes and rotate on every refresh; replayed tokens revoke the session.

6. Retention and deletion

Account rows persist while the account exists. Guest sessions expire after 180 days of inactivity. Delete anytime in-app (Settings > Delete account) or by emailing support@duoorb.com with your handle + user ID. Backups age out within 90 days. See Terms and Contact.

7. Your rights

You can edit your display name, username, bio and avatar at any time in Settings, and request access, correction, export or deletion of your data at any time via Contact. Material practice changes will be noted here with a new date before taking effect.

8. Children

Not directed at children under 13. Contact us to remove any such data.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the in-app links when practices change. For questions or requests, see Contact.
