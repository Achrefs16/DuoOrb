Post-Match Breakdown

How Every Move Gets Graded, From Best to Blunder

After each match, DuoOrb replays every turn and scores how much win chance it gained or lost you — then labels it in plain words, from Best down to Blunder:

  • Best (Loss ≤ 0.03): The mathematically optimal path step or highest-leverage wall on the board.
  • Excellent (Loss ≤ 0.07): A near-optimal decision that preserves full positional command.
  • Good (Loss ≤ 0.15): A solid, practical move (guaranteed whenever move accuracy retains ≥ 85% win equity).
  • Forced: The only sensible legal response in a single-corridor sequence.
  • Inaccuracy (Loss ≤ 0.28): A suboptimal wall or passive step that yields 1–2 steps of tempo.
  • Mistake (Loss ≤ 0.42): A serious tactical slip that hands control of the board state to your opponent.
  • Blunder (Loss greater than 0.42): A game-swinging error — such as sealing the wrong wing of the board or walking into a multi-wall trap.
