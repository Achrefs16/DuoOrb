1. DuoOrb — The Gold Standard for Grid Tactics

If you love the pathfinding tension of Quoridor, DuoOrb is the definitive mobile implementation. Officially available on Google Play, it pairs tactical 2-square wall placements with an astonishing 39 offline AI bot personalities rated from 450 (friendly beginner) to 2900 ELO (Grandmaster alpha-beta solver).

Even better: matches are 100% free of mid-game ads, and every game can be analyzed using its built-in Rust Monte Carlo Tree Search review engine that pinpoints exact blunders and turning points.

2. The Battle of Polytopia — Pocket 4X Mastery

For players who want larger tactical scope, Polytopia distills the complexity of games like Civilization into an elegant 30-turn mobile format. You explore the map, farm resources, research technologies, and conquer rival tribes.

3. Onitama — Dynamic Movement Cards

Set on a compact 5×5 shrine grid, Onitama introduces a brilliant card-rotation mechanism. Players maneuver their Master and Student pawns using five animal movement cards that pass between opponents upon each turn.

4. Hive with AI — Pure Tactile Enclosure

Unlike grid-based games, Hive uses hexagonal insect pieces placed directly on the table. Each insect moves differently (Grasshoppers leap, Ants patrol the perimeter, and Beetles climb on top of other pieces) in an intense race to surround the opposing Queen Bee.
