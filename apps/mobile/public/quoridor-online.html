Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Quoridor and how is DuoOrb related?

Quoridor is an award-winning abstract strategy board game where players race to the opposite edge while placing walls to slow down opponents. DuoOrb modernizes this classic formula with online multiplayer, custom board sizes, Center Rush and Race modes, multi-level AI bots, and real-time clock controls.

Can I play DuoOrb online without installing an app?

Yes! DuoOrb runs entirely in any modern browser on PC, Mac, iPhone, and Android. You can play as a guest immediately with zero downloads.

Can you completely block an opponent from winning?

No. The golden rule of the game dictates that you can never completely trap an opponent. At least one open path to the goal line must always remain available.

How does the jump rule work?

When two orbs face each other with no wall between them, you can jump directly over your opponent into the square beyond. If a wall blocks the square behind them, you may jump diagonally to either side.
