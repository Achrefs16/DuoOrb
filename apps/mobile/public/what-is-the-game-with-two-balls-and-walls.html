What is the Game with Two Balls and Walls?

Direct Answer: The viral board game featuring two spheres racing across a square grid while players place flat wooden walls into grooves is Quoridor (and in its modern, competitive digital version, DuoOrb). You race your orb to the opposite edge of a 9×9 board while placing 2-square walls to trap and detour your opponent.

How the Core Rules Work

1. Move or Wall

Every turn is an either/or choice: advance your orb one orthogonal step (North, South, East, West), or drop one wall from your budget of 10.

2. Jump Mechanics

When two orbs collide face to face, the active player can leap over to gain double tempo. Walls behind the opponent force diagonal jump escapes.

3. Path Guarantee

It is illegal to place a wall that leaves either player with zero legal paths to their destination row. BFS algorithms validate every placement.
