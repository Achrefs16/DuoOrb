About DuoOrb

DuoOrb is Quoridor-style strategy: race orbs, block with walls.

DuoOrb by AS Digital is our modern take on Quoridor — the classic 9×9 duel where two orbs race across the board while players drop walls to slow each other down. It is officially live on Google Play for Android and playable instantly in any modern web browser for iOS and desktop. One account, one rating, everywhere.

Engineering & Architecture

Built for Speed, Fair Play and Everyone

AS Digital created DuoOrb for one reason: the classic corridor duel deserved the same serious infrastructure as chess — a real rating ladder, instant friend lobbies, and an engine that teaches you after every loss. No luck mechanics, no pay-to-win, no ads inside matches. Just the duel, engineered to be fast, fair, and playable by anyone in ten languages.

Server Authority

Cheat-Proof Rules

Every online move, jump, clock tick and wall check is validated on our server. Cheating tools, illegal walls and sealed-board tricks get rejected before they touch your game.

10 Languages

Global Accessibility

Strategy belongs to everyone. The game itself plays in ten languages — English, Arabic (full RTL layout and custom typography), German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Turkish.

Pure Focus

Ad-Free During Matches

Players are never interrupted mid-calculation. All 39 AI personalities and all online game modes are free to play with fluid 60 FPS performance.
