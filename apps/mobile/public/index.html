Anatomy of a Turn

Sixty Seconds to Your First Match

One tap opens the arena. From there every turn works the same way, whether you face a friend, a rated stranger, or a starter bot. Details: AI & Analysis.

Step 1

Step or Build

Move your orb one square — or spend the turn placing one wall from your reserve into the grooves. Walls are permanent, so each one is a real decision.

Step 2

Jump When Close

Meet face-to-face with no wall between you and you leap straight over your rival — or diagonally if a wall, the board edge, or a third orb blocks the landing square.

Step 3

Race, Review, Climb

Touch the far edge (or the center tile in Center Rush) to win. Then open Engine Review to see every move graded, and queue ranked to climb the ladder.

Play With Friends

How Private Room Codes Work

Tap Create Room and you get a short 6-letter code. Send it — or the join link — over any chat app, and your friend is playing with you in seconds, no account needed. You pick the mode, walls and clock, then you both hit Ready.

Rating, Simply

How the Rating Ladder Works

Every rated 1v1 match moves one number: your rating, starting at 1500. Win and it climbs — faster when you're new, steadier once you've proven yourself. The leaderboard shows exactly where you stand.

From Lobby to Review

How an Online Match Flows

1 · Lobby

Create or Queue

Generate a room code for a friend, or enter rated matchmaking. The host sets the mode, wall budget and clock before both sides mark Ready.

2 · Duel

Play the Clock

Alternate move-or-wall turns under your chosen control. Illegal walls — overlaps, crosses or zero-path seals — are rejected before they land.

3 · Review

Grade Every Turn

Win or lose, Engine Review scores each turn from Best to Blunder and names the deciding moment. Rematch in one tap.

60-Second Identification Guide

Looking for the Viral Game with Two Balls and Wooden Walls?

People search for this classic under dozens of names — from “the two marbles and wooden fences game” to “the 9×9 corridor block game.” Match these five signatures against the clip you watched:

What You Saw in the Clip Actual Game Term in DuoOrb How the Mechanic Works
Two orbs starting at opposite ends of a square grid Orbs on the 9×9 Arena Blue starts on bottom center e9 and races to the top edge; Red starts on top center e1 and races to the bottom edge.
Players sliding flat wooden planks into slots between squares 2-Square Groove Walls Each player starts a 1v1 duel with 10 walls. Placing a wall takes your entire turn and permanently blocks two adjacent square edges.
One orb hopping straight or diagonally over the other orb Straight & Diagonal Jumps Face-to-face with no wall between you, leap straight over — or diagonally if a wall blocks behind. Full rules: Rules.
A player forced to walk all the way back across a giant zig-zag maze The Golden Path Rule You can never seal an opponent in completely (at least 1 open path must always exist), but you can force a 25-step detour across the board.
Four different colored orbs racing toward the middle of the board 4-Player Center Rush DuoOrb's party mode where 4 players start on all four edges with 5 walls each and race to claim the single center square.
Multiplayer Questions

Online Play, Answered

Can I play online without installing anything?

Yes. DuoOrb runs in any modern browser on phone or computer. Play as a guest immediately — zero downloads.

How do private rooms work?

Create a room, share the 6-letter code or link, and your friend joins instantly. No account needed for guest play.

How does rated matchmaking work?

Queue for a rated 1v1 duel on one universal rating from 1500. Wins move you up the global leaderboard.

What is DuoOrb's relationship to classic corridor strategy?

DuoOrb is our modern take on the classic 9×9 wall-placement duel: same race-and-block heart, plus online rooms, clocks, bots and engine review. New to the rules? Start at Rules.
