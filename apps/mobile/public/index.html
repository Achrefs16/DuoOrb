Three rules. Endless depth.

Simple enough to learn in one turn. Deep enough to challenge master strategists.

1. Move or Wall

Advance your orb one square in any cardinal direction, or spend your turn dropping a permanent 2-square wall from your reserve.

2. Jump When Blocked

When face to face with your opponent, leap straight over. If a wall or board edge blocks the landing, jump diagonally.

3. The Golden Rule

You can force a 25-step detour across the labyrinth, but sealing an opponent completely without a path to the goal is strictly illegal.
